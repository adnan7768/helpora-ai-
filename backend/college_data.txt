1. MES OVERVIEW
 
Q. About MES
 
A. The Muslim Educational Society, established in 1964 achieved a remarkable transformation in the educational status of Muslim and other backward communities. It was a socially committed and timely venture of a group of intellectuals led by Dr. P.K. Abdul Ghafoor, the Founder President of the Muslim Educational Society who dedicated his life for an educational revolution and pledged to keep the lamp of knowledge aloft to illuminate the life of a society.

2. MES AIMAT INSTITUTION OVERVIEW
Q. What is AIMAT?
 
A. AIMAT stands for MES Advanced Institute of Management and Technology. It is a postgraduate professional institution located in Aluva, Kerala.
 
Q.Where is AIMAT located?
 
A. AIMAT is located at Marampally P.O., Aluva – 683105, Ernakulam District, Kerala, India.
 
Q. When was AIMAT established?
     A. AIMAT was established in 2001 under the management of the Muslim Educational Society (MES).
Q. Which university is AIMAT affiliated with?
 
A.   AIMAT is affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University, Kerala.
Q. Is AIMAT approved by AICTE?
   
   A.  Yes, AIMAT is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

Q. What is the vision of AIMAT?

 A. AIMAT aims to provide quality professional education with strong ethical values, leadership skills
    and industry-oriented knowledge.
Q.  What is the mission of AIMAT?

A.   The mission is to develop competent professionals through academic excellence, research
       orientation, innovation, and personality development.

4. ELIGIBILITY & ADMISSION

Q: What is the eligibility for MCA?
A: Candidates must have a Bachelor’s Degree with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, as per University norms.

Q: What is the eligibility for MBA?
A: Candidates must have a Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline with the required minimum marks as per University regulations.

Q: Which entrance exams are accepted for MBA?
A: MBA admissions are based on valid scores from KMAT, CMAT, MAT, or as per University norms.
 
Q. Which entrance exams are accepted for MCA ?
A. MCA admissions are based on Entrance exam conducted by LBS

Q: How can I apply for admission?
A: Students must apply through official admission procedures notified by the institution and the University.

Q: Is admission merit-based?
A: Yes, admission follows entrance examination scores and University guidelines.

11. MES FACILITIES
 
Q. What facilities does MES AIMAT campus offer?
 
A. MES AIMAT offers a wide range of facilities including: Auditorium, Hostel, Well-equipped classrooms, College bus, Health club, Computer labs, Refreshment zone/canteen, Library with National Digital Library access, Dedicated placement cells, E-Library, and Wi-Fi connectivity.
 
Q. Does MES AIMAT have a placement cell?
A.Yes, MES AIMAT has dedicated placement cells for both the MBA and MCA programmes. The placement cell provides career guidance, job opportunities, and placement support. Companies like Choice, Bajaj Life Goals, and Keerthi Nirmal have visited the campus for recruitment drives. The college has a strong placement record.
 
Q.Which companies visit MES AIMAT for placement?

Q. Does MES AIMAT provide placement training?
A.Yes, MES AIMAT conducts Placement Training Programmes for students. 
 
Q. What clubs and student support services does MES AIMAT have?
